Why Choose Us & Fleet Overview

As a local Jamaican family business, D&C Refuse Trucking & Equipment Co. Limited treats every job with personal care and attention. We take great pride in our punctuality, transparent communication, and ensuring your goods and waste are transported safely every time across Jamaica.

Commercial box and refuse trucks outfitted with heavy-duty hydraulic tail-lifts for effortless cargo removal, haulage, and waste management across Jamaica.

  • 🚚 Hydraulic Tail Lift Equipped: Safe and simple loading for heavy equipment, machinery, and commercial pallets.
  • 📦 7.5-Ton / 20ft Enclosed Transport: Weatherproof private truck hire, house removals, and furniture moving to protect cargo during long-distance delivery.
  • 🛠️ Commercial Waste Management: Scheduled refuse collection, site clearance, and waste removal for businesses and developments.
  • ⚡ 24/7 Emergency Haulage: Always ready around the clock for emergency dispatch islandwide.
